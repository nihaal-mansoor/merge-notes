Dubai data, checked

Dubai property, from the official data

Every sale and rent contract in Dubai is registered with the Dubai Land Department, and much of that data is public. I work through it with Claude Code, the same way I build software: one question at a time, with every step written down.

Then I check every figure against the official indexes before it goes up. When a number doesn't match an official source, it stays out until I've tested it and can explain the gap. I learned that the hard way, when an analysis passed two checks and still didn't hold up against the official index.

How I check the numbers

  • Public data only. Never my employer's data, listings or projects.
  • Every figure has a source and an exact period. If you can't see where a number came from, it doesn't go up.
  • Like with like. Sales-only figures are never compared with totals that include mortgages and gifts.
  • Checked against the official index. A finding that disagrees with it waits until I can test it.
  • The numbers, not predictions. I report what the data shows. I don't tell anyone where or when to buy.
