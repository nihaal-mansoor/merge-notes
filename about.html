Who I am

Hi, I'm Nihaal.

I'm a developer at a real-estate company in Dubai.

In January 2026, my biggest job was a WordPress theme. Ten months later I look after eight apps: the lead dashboard, a calling app for the sales team, a lead router, an HR and admin portal, landing pages, a booking app for content creators, the company website, and a CRM rebuild that's on the way.

I build them with Claude Code. Claude writes most of the code. I decide what gets built and why, what "done" means and what goes live. I read every plan and I merge every pull request.

How I think about the work

Building things is the easy part these days. The hard part is figuring out how people will use what you build. The people who use my tools are salespeople, not tech people. Their job is to sell, not to learn my system. So if a page needs a paragraph to explain itself, the page is wrong.

AI doesn't really build the wrong thing. It misunderstands when I keep things vague, and that's on me. You still need some coding knowledge to make sure what it builds works as intended.

When a train runs on time, nobody thinks about the driver. Most of my work is like that. When it goes well, nobody notices. This site is where I write about it anyway.
